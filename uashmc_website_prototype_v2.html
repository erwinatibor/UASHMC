About UASHMC

Healthcare Built Around You

"Excellence in Every Life We Touch."

United Antipolo Specialists Hospital and Medical Center (UASHMC) is a private specialists' hospital along Marcos Highway in Antipolo City, bringing consultations, laboratory, imaging, surgery and maternity care together on one site.

We bring together physicians across major medical disciplines to provide personalized care for you and your family.
